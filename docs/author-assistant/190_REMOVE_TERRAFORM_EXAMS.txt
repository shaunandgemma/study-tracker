190. REMOVE THE TWO TERRAFORM EXAMS

1. Scope
Remove only the old browser-created custom exams named "Terraform Associate" and "Terraform Associate Certification".

2. Storage location
These were browser-local custom exams stored under exampulse_exams_v1. They were not built-in exam files and were not Supabase exam records.

3. Removal behaviour
When the app loads, it removes those two exact exam titles from browser storage. Their local checklist, flagged-question and exam-history records are also removed. If one was selected, the app safely returns to AWS SAA-C03.

4. Preserved content
All other exams remain. Terraform Follow Alongs, including the CloudFormation and Terraform Follow Along, remain unchanged.

5. Prevention
Old backups or browser data cannot restore either retired custom exam into the visible exam list. A separately maintained built-in Terraform Associate (004) exam may use its own unique ID and official title.

6. Verification
The focused exam-navigation tests, linter and production build pass. Existing unrelated linter warnings remain unchanged.

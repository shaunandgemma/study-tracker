Do not create a release candidate from the separate revision 1 draft.

Do not delete either Lambda draft until the exact original and unintended draft IDs, ownership, revision, and candidate state have been checked.

1. Verify both Lambda Shared Draft rows read-only.
2. Confirm which revision 1 draft came from the new handoff fingerprint.
3. Confirm that the unintended draft has no release candidate.
4. Remove only the verified unintended revision 1 draft after explicit approval.
5. Restart or fully refresh the Author application.
6. Select Shared Drafts.

8. Confirm that the comparison shows revision 84 changing to revision 85.
9. Apply the update only after that comparison is correct.

8. UPDATE-ROUTE CORRECTION REQUIRED

The controlled update route must identify the original draft from the published candidate ID, not only from the programme ID.

If the Shared Draft revision is newer than the published revision, it must compare the current draft with the published candidate snapshot before allowing an update.

If the content differs, the update must stop and require a new baseline.

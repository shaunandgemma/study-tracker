203. TROUBLESHOOTING CHALLENGES AND INCIDENT NOTEBOOK

1. Purpose
Add a separate practical Troubleshooting section where learners diagnose realistic faults without following a step-by-step walkthrough.

2. Exam availability
The Troubleshooting card is available from the local AWS SAA-C03 and Terraform Associate 004 exam landing pages.

3. Terraform challenges
1. Repair a Terraform validation failure.
2. Stop an unintended EC2 replacement.
3. Reconcile resource drift safely.

4. AWS challenges
1. Restore private-subnet outbound connectivity.
2. Recover unhealthy Application Load Balancer targets.
3. Diagnose an Amazon S3 AccessDenied response.

5. Independent content
Each of the six challenges has its own editable JavaScript file under src/data/troubleshootingChallenges.
The shared catalogue preserves exam assignment and display order.

6. Learner workflow
Each incident provides a scenario, task, starting evidence, success criteria, optional progressive hints, diagnosis questions, a validated resolution, and prevention guidance.

7. New quality-of-life tool
The Incident Notebook and RCA Builder lets learners pin relevant evidence, record observations, write a working hypothesis, record attempted actions, and copy a structured incident report.
Notebook work, progress, scores and completion are saved automatically in local browser storage on the learner's computer.

8. Scoring
An unassisted correct diagnosis scores 100.
Each revealed hint reduces the available score by 10, with a minimum successful score of 70.
Ending the challenge and revealing the solution records an assisted score of 0.

9. Safety and isolation
The challenges are local simulations. They do not connect to AWS, Terraform, Supabase or Author and cannot change infrastructure.
Existing Follow Alongs, Knowledge Guides and practice exams remain separate.

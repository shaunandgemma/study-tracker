191. TERRAFORM ASSOCIATE 004 EXAM OPTION

1. Exam
The app now includes HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate (004) as a built-in exam option.

2. Official basis
The exam definition follows HashiCorp's current Terraform Associate 004 certification page and published objective list. The assessed Terraform product version is 1.12 and the official duration is 60 minutes.

3. Checklist
The exam has eight objective groups and all 37 published sub-objectives as independently checkable items.

4. Practice exam
The exam includes an initial local bank of 16 original practice questions, with two questions for every objective group.

5. Follow Along
The controlled CloudFormation and Terraform Follow Along is assigned to this exam instead of AWS SAA-C03 when its published programme ID is cloudformation-terraform-learning-path.

6. Preservation
AWS SAA-C03, CompTIA Security+, other exams, and all unrelated Follow Alongs remain unchanged.

7. Full Mock correction
Terraform Full Mock uses all 16 available Terraform questions and does not apply the AWS SAA-C03 65-question domain allocation. The card shows 16 questions and the Terraform 60-minute duration. Retakes use the same exam-specific rule.
